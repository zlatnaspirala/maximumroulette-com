Attributions & Credits

The Credits, Intellectual References, and Licensing index

WebGPU Engine Resources 01 // MEWGPU

The build layers, procedural pipeline layouts, and buffer management processes running within matrix-engine-wgpu leverage, reference, and adapt pioneering code from the following high precision open-source repositories:

WebGPU Samples (Google Chrome Labs)

The shadow mapping orchestration and scene structure logic adapt implementation concepts from the official WebGPU Samples Shading Suite.

WGP-GLTF Library

The custom .gltf rendering and mesh parsing functions adapt designs from Twinklebear's webgpu-gltf parser, expanded with AI optimization assistance.

Ray Tracing & Graphics Book Guides

The basic OBJ layout structures are modeled from the Mathematical HWS Cube-Camera WebGL examples, while the raw mathematical Ray Casting steps are inspired by the Maier Felix WebGPU Ray Tracing Index.

Legacy WebGL Project Resources 02 // WEBGL

Historical core elements of the original WebGL matrix-engine architecture carry forward foundations, maths, and shaders designed by these contributors:

Keestu GPL Framework Foundation

The overarching foundation and base pipeline structuring was originally modeled on designs by Tappali Ekanathan Keestu (C) 2014 under the GNU General Public License.

Glow Shaders & WebGL2 Fundamentals

The shadow GLSL mathematics and fragment shader structures map logic from WebGL2 Fundamentals and the WebGL Frag Lens Shader collection.

WebM & Fractal video media

Video sampler streams (such as active loop textures) are derived from "Electric Sheep" animated flame fractals created by TheMrNgaard (under Creative Commons Attribution licenses).
